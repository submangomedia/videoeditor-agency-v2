If you need to know how we handle your information right now, please ask us on the contact page.

This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit this website or get in touch with us.

1. Who we are

Video Editor Agency is a trading name of Mango Media Digital, based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Mango Media Digital is the data controller for the information described in this policy — that means we are the ones who decide why and how it is used, and we are responsible for it.

[[R30 — FULL REGISTERED ADDRESS: street, area, Dhaka, postcode. A data controller must be identifiable. "Dhaka, Bangladesh" is not enough on this page, even though it is enough on /contact/.]]

You can reach us about anything in this policy at videoeditoragency.hello@gmail.com.

2. The short version

This website has no analytics, no advertising trackers and no cookie banner, because it sets no cookies of its own and does not track you. It is plain HTML and CSS with no JavaScript. Two things do involve someone other than us, and they are described in §5 and §6.

3. Information you give us

There is currently no contact form on this website. If you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we receive whatever you choose to send — typically your name, your contact details, and whatever you tell us about your project.

We use that information only to:

  • reply to you;
  • prepare a quote or a proposal;
  • deliver work you have asked us to do; and
  • keep our own records of what was agreed.

We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone for marketing. We do not add you to a mailing list because you enquired.

4. Footage and files you send us

If you become a client you will send us video footage and related files. Those files sometimes contain personal information — faces, voices, names, and occasionally information about people other than you.

We treat that material as yours. We use it only to produce the work you have commissioned, we do not use it to train anything, and we do not publish any of it as a portfolio piece without asking you first.

[[RETENTION: how long do we keep client footage and project files after delivery? A real number — "90 days after final delivery, then deleted" — or a real policy. "As long as necessary" is what every template says and it means nothing.]]

5. Fonts loaded from Google

This site uses the Inter typeface, which is loaded from Google Fonts. That means your browser requests the font files from Google when the page loads, and Google receives your IP address as part of that request. This happens automatically on every page, and we do not receive or store any of that information ourselves.

Google’s handling of that request is covered by Google’s privacy policy. If you would rather this did not happen, most browsers and content blockers can block requests to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com; the site will still work and will simply use a standard system typeface instead.

6. Video on the portfolio page

Our portfolio page shows still images of our work. Those images are hosted by us. If you click one to play the video, it loads from YouTube at that point, and YouTube may then set cookies and receive information about your visit under Google’s own privacy policy. Nothing is loaded from YouTube unless you click.

7. Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information needed to deliver the page to you — including your IP address — and keeps short-term logs for security and reliability. We do not use those logs to identify or profile visitors.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to how we use it, or to have it sent to someone else. If you are in the UK or the EU, these rights come from the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR.

To exercise any of them, contact us using the details on the contact page. We will respond within one month.

[[COMPLAINTS: if a UK or EU visitor is unhappy with our response, they can complain to their national data protection authority. A lawyer should confirm whether this policy needs to name a specific one, and whether an EU or UK representative is required given that we are outside both.]]

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information — for example, if we add a contact form — we will update this page and change the date at the top of it.

See also our terms and conditions.
