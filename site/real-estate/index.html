Video Editing for Real Estate

Property tours, agent introductions and market updates — edited so a listing looks like the price you are asking for it.

Get started See our work

The problem

The listing went up. The video did not.

  • The tour is shot and then it sits

    By the time it is cut, the property is under offer and the video was never part of the sale.

  • Phone footage prices the house down

    A shaky walkthrough on a listing at the top of the market tells a buyer something you did not intend to say.

  • Drone footage is the expensive part and it is used worst

    Sixty seconds of aerial with no cut, no pacing and no reason to keep watching.

  • You are the brand, and you are on camera least

    The agent intro and the market update are what wins the next instruction — and they are the videos that never get made.

How we work

Every listing edited to the standard of your best one

In real estate the video is not marketing collateral produced after the sale. It is part of the listing, it goes out on a deadline, and it is compared side by side with every other agent's on the same portal and the same feed.

Video Editor Agency edits the whole set — the walkthrough, the vertical cut for social, the agent piece to camera. The same editor handles every listing, so your tenth looks like your first. Revisions are genuinely unlimited, which matters when a vendor wants the kitchen shot moved forward.

What we deliver

What we edit for agents and agencies

  • Property tours and walkthroughs

    Paced so a viewer reaches the last room.

  • Drone and aerial footage cut into the tour

    Establishing the location, not filling the runtime.

  • Vertical listing cuts

    The same property for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, cut vertical rather than cropped.

  • Agent introduction videos

    The piece to camera that wins the instruction.

  • Market update content

    The recurring format that keeps you in front of sellers between listings.

  • Thumbnails and titles for listing videos

    The frame that decides whether the tour gets watched.
