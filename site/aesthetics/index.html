Treatment content, results and clinic storytelling — edited to the standard the treatment is priced at.

The problem

In aesthetics, the video is the consultation

  • The result is the whole pitch and the footage does not do it justice

    Different light, different angle, different day — and the change that is obvious in the room is invisible on camera.

  • Every clinic's feed looks the same

    The same treatment, the same stock line, the same music. A patient scrolling cannot tell any of you apart.

  • Ads get rejected and nobody knows which frame did it

    The creative was approved internally, and the platform disagreed.

  • The clinic is priced as premium and edited as budget

    Nothing undoes a treatment price faster than content that looks like it was made on a phone at the reception desk.

How we work

Content that matches what the treatment costs

Aesthetics is a considered purchase. A patient researches for weeks, compares clinics, and decides largely on what they can see. Your video is doing the work a consultation would — before anyone has walked in.

Video Editor Agency edits the treatment content, the clinic film and the short-form to one consistent standard, with the same editor across everything so the feed reads as one clinic. Where results footage is involved, we cut a compliant version alongside the organic one, so the campaign is not held up by a rejection. Revisions are genuinely unlimited.

What we deliver

What we edit for clinics

  • Treatment and procedure content

    The process explained, cut for a patient rather than a practitioner.

  • Results and transformation edits

    Colour and exposure matched so the change reads as the change, not as the lighting.

  • Compliant ad variants

    A second cut built for platform review alongside the organic one.

  • Clinic and practitioner films

    The room, the team, the reason to choose you.

  • Short-form for Reels, TikTok and Shorts

    Cut vertical, captioned, one point each.

  • Motion graphics and on-screen text

    Treatment names, aftercare, the detail a patient forgets by the car park.
