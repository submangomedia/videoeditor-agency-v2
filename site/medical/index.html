Video Editing for Doctors & Medical Practices

Patient education, clinic content and pieces to camera — edited to carry the authority the subject deserves.

Get started See our work

The problem

Your expertise is the hardest thing to put on camera

  • The explanation takes twenty minutes and nobody watches twenty minutes

    The information is right. The format is losing.

  • Production quality reads as competence

    A patient deciding between two practices is not qualified to judge your medicine. They will judge what they can see.

  • The footage is recorded between clinics and it shows

    Interruptions, restarts, an answered phone — the good material is in there, buried in an hour of it.

  • The person who should be editing is you, and you have a list

    Which is why the channel has four videos and the last one is from March.

How we work

The explanation you already give, made watchable

Patient education is the rare kind of video where the goal is comprehension rather than reach. Someone came looking for an answer. Whether they get it — and whether they trust the person giving it — is decided in the edit as much as in the recording.

Video Editor Agency cuts the recording down to the answer, adds the on-screen support the explanation needs, and holds the same treatment across every video so the practice looks like one practice. Revisions are genuinely unlimited, which matters when a clinical detail has to be worded exactly.

What we deliver

What we edit for medical practices

  • Patient education videos

    The long explanation, cut to the length someone will actually watch.

  • Pieces to camera

    The recording tightened, the restarts removed, the pauses closed.

  • Motion graphics for the parts that need showing

    The diagram that does what narration cannot.

  • Clinic and practice content

    The introduction, the facility, the team.

  • Short-form answers

    The single common question, cut vertical for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

  • Captions and on-screen terminology

    Spelled correctly, and on screen long enough to read.
