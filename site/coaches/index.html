Four things that quietly cap a coaching business

  • The edit eats the week

    Hours that should go into coaching, curriculum, or sales calls go into cutting footage instead.

  • The course never launches

    The material is recorded. It sits on a drive because turning it into finished modules feels like starting a second business.

  • Posting is inconsistent

    Content goes out in bursts when there is time, and stops when there isn't — so the audience never builds momentum.

  • The production quality undercuts the price

    A programme sold at premium rates, presented in video that looks like it was assembled in a hurry, works against the rate you are asking for.

Your content should work as hard as you do

Coaches who publish consistently attract more clients, command higher rates, and build authority that compounds. Doing that at volume while actually running a coaching practice is a second full-time job — and it is the one that always gets dropped first.

Video Editor Agency takes the whole post-production workflow off your desk. You get a dedicated editor who learns how you teach, and a project manager who keeps the schedule moving. You record. We finish. Revisions are genuinely unlimited — we are done when you say we are done.
