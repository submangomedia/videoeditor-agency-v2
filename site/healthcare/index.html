Four reasons clinic video stalls

  • Recording is easy. Finishing is not.

    A phone and ten minutes between patients produces the footage. The edit is what sits undone for a month.

  • Auto-captions get the terms wrong

    A caption that misspells a procedure or a medication undermines the person saying it. Medical captions need a human pass.

  • Before-and-after footage needs judgement

    Framing, blur, consent and platform rules all apply. It is not the same as cutting a vlog.

  • The brand drifts between posts

    Different fonts, colours and lower-thirds every week make a practice look smaller than it is.

You see patients. We finish the video.

Send the raw recording and a note on what it is for. Your dedicated editor learns your specialty's vocabulary, your clinic's look and the platforms you post on, and returns finished content that reads as yours.

Revisions are genuinely unlimited. If a term is wrong, a frame should be blurred, or a claim needs softening, it is changed until you sign it off.

We edit clinical and aesthetic content; we do not advise on medical advertising rules in your jurisdiction. Where a caption or a claim needs a clinician's or a regulator's eye, we flag it and follow your call.

Questions clinics ask

  • Do you understand medical terminology?

    Your editor works from your transcript and your brief, checks every term against them, and asks when unsure. Nothing technical is guessed.

  • Can you handle patient footage confidentially?

    Yes. Footage is shared through a private folder, seen only by your assigned editor, and deleted from our systems on request after delivery. Tell us your requirements in the brief.

  • Will you blur faces or remove identifying details?

    Yes, to the rule you set — full blur, partial, or crop. It is part of the edit, not an add-on.

  • Can the same edit run as an ad?

    Usually. We export ad-sized variants from the same master and cut a shorter hook for paid placements. Media buying itself is not something this site sells.

  • Do you edit for clinics outside Bangladesh?

    Yes — editing is fully remote. Only on-site recording is limited to Dhaka.
