## 6. The new audience pages — one section each

### 6.1 `/healthcare/` — doctors, dentists, dermatologists, aesthetic clinics

- **Why one page, not four:** zero catalogued items for three of the four specialties.
  Four pages would be four near-identical pages with one showreel between them.
  Sub-headings per specialty inside one page give each a heading and a portfolio anchor
  without a URL that has nothing behind it. Split later, one at a time, on proof.
- **Buyer:** a clinic owner or practitioner making patient-education, procedure-explainer,
  before/after, and testimonial content; usually short-form; often running Meta ads
  (which is why §7 cross-links here).
- **Pain points (draft):** filming is the easy part, editing is what never gets done ·
  medical content needs captions that are *accurate*, not auto-generated · before/after
  and procedure footage needs tasteful handling, blur and consent-aware cuts · platform
  rules on medical claims mean every caption is a liability.
- **Deliverables (draft):** captioned short-form for Reels/TikTok/Shorts · long-form
  explainers · thumbnail sets · Meta ad variants (→ `/ads/`) · consistent on-brand
  lower-thirds.
- **Claims to avoid:** anything about patient outcomes, "more patients", "more
  appointments". Tasty Edits prints "403% more new patients" with no source. VEA prints
  nothing of the kind. **Aesthetic and dermatology before/after content is regulated
  advertising in the UK, EU, Australia and parts of the US** — VEA edits it; VEA does
  not advise on it. One sentence saying so protects the page. *(INFERENCE, T11 — not
  legal advice; Masud may want a line from a lawyer if this vertical grows.)*
- **Proof today:** 1 showreel. `[[see §5 row 1]]`
- **Differentiator vs Tasty Edits, honestly:** price; dermatology/aesthetic-specific
  proof *if it arrives*; motion graphics in-house for procedure explainers (they exclude
  2D/3D animation).

### 6.2 `/legal/` — lawyers, law firms, legal businesses

- **Buyer:** a solo practitioner or small firm building a personal brand — talking-head
  explainers, podcast clips, "what to do if…" shorts, and Meta/YouTube ads for intake.
- **Pain points (draft):** lawyers speak in long, careful sentences that short-form
  punishes · every clip is a potential advertising-rules problem (bar rules vary by
  jurisdiction) · a firm's brand has to look conservative *and* stop the scroll ·
  consistency across dozens of episodes.
- **Deliverables (draft):** podcast-to-shorts pipelines (this is the one thing VEA can
  prove at volume today — 13 episodes for one firm) · long-form episode edits ·
  captions checked against the transcript · intake-ad variants (→ `/ads/`).
- **Claims to avoid:** "more clients", "more consultations", any result. Same reason as
  6.1. **Legal advertising is regulated; VEA edits, VEA does not advise.**
- **Proof today:** 13 items, one client, unnamed. Enough for the page to exist, honestly
  titled. `[[≥2 more legal clients would make it a practice, not a relationship]]`
- **Differentiator:** proven podcast-to-shorts workflow at volume; price.
