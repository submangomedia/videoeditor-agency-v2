## 2. The problem

**H2** — The listing went up. The video did not.

- **The tour is shot and then it sits.** By the time it is cut, the property is under
  offer and the video was never part of the sale.
- **Phone footage prices the house down.** A shaky walkthrough on a listing at the top of
  the market tells a buyer something you did not intend to say.
- **Drone footage is the expensive part and it is used worst.** Sixty seconds of aerial
  with no cut, no pacing and no reason to keep watching.
- **You are the brand, and you are on camera least.** The agent intro and the market
  update are what wins the next instruction — and they are the videos that never get made.

---

## 3. The solution

**H2** — Every listing edited to the standard of your best one

**Paragraph 1** — In real estate the video is not marketing collateral produced after the
sale. It is part of the listing, it goes out on a deadline, and it is compared side by side
with every other agent's on the same portal and the same feed.

**Paragraph 2** — Video Editor Agency edits the whole set — the walkthrough, the vertical
cut for social, the agent piece to camera. The same editor handles every listing, so your
tenth looks like your first. Revisions are genuinely unlimited, which matters when a vendor
wants the kitchen shot moved forward.

---

## 4. What we deliver

**H2** — What we edit for agents and agencies

- **Property tours and walkthroughs** — paced so a viewer reaches the last room.
- **Drone and aerial footage cut into the tour** — establishing the location, not filling
  the runtime.
- **Vertical listing cuts** — the same property for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, cut vertical
  rather than cropped.
- **Agent introduction videos** — the piece to camera that wins the instruction.
- **Market update content** — the recurring format that keeps you in front of sellers
  between listings.
- **Thumbnails and titles for listing videos** — the frame that decides whether the tour
  gets watched.
