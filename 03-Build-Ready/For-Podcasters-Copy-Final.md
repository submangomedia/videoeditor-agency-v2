## 2. The problem

**H2** — Recording the episode was the easy part

- **The edit is longer than the episode.** Two hours of multi-cam takes far longer than
  two hours to cut, and it lands on whoever in the team has the least time.
- **The clips never get made.** Everyone knows the short-form is where the growth is. It is
  also the job that gets dropped when the episode itself is late.
- **The video version is an afterthought.** Audio goes out clean; the video goes out as a
  static waveform or a single locked-off shot, and it performs like one.
- **Every episode looks slightly different.** Different intro, different captions,
  different lower thirds — because whoever cut it that week did it their way.

---

## 3. The solution

**H2** — One recording in. A week of content out.

**Paragraph 1** — A podcast is not one piece of content. It is a long-form episode, a set
of short-form clips, a thumbnail, and a set of assets that have to look like they came
from the same show. Most podcasts publish the first one and never get to the rest.

**Paragraph 2** — Video Editor Agency takes the whole recording and returns the set. The
same editor cuts every episode, so the intro, the captions and the lower thirds stay
identical week to week. Revisions are genuinely unlimited — a show that publishes on a
schedule cannot afford an edit that stalls.

---

## 4. What we deliver

**H2** — What we edit for podcasts

- **Multi-cam episode editing** — speaker switching cut to the conversation, not to a timer.
- **Short-form clips pulled from the episode** — vertical, captioned, cut for TikTok,
  Reels and Shorts rather than cropped from the wide.
- **Branded lower thirds, intro and outro** — the same set every week.
- **Chapter markers** — so a two-hour episode is navigable instead of a wall.
- **Audio cleaned and levelled** — room noise, mismatched mic levels, the cough in take three.
- **Thumbnail design** — per episode, A/B-ready.
