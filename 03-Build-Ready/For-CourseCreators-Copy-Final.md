## ⚠️ Read this before the copy. This page has to earn its separation from `/coaches/`.

`EXPANSION-v1.md` §5 flags this as the closest pair on the site. Course editing is
**deliverable #1 on `/coaches/`**, and `videoeditor_agency_website_copy` PAGE 7 already
sells coaches on *"online courses not getting launched because editing feels

chose one.

**The split this copy commits to, and it must be held in any future edit:**

| | `/coaches/` | `/course-creators/` |
|---|---|---|
| What they sell | **their time** — 1:1 and group coaching | **the video itself** — the course IS the product |
| Video's job | fill the calendar, build authority | **be** the thing the customer paid for |
| What failure costs | fewer enquiries | refunds, bad reviews, non-completion |

**That is the whole distinction.** For a coach, a rough edit is a marketing problem. For a
course creator, a rough edit is a **product defect in something someone paid for.** Every
line below leans on that. If a later edit softens it toward "we edit your videos", the two
pages collapse and become the duplicate-content problem `THREAD-PLAN.md` §5 T7 warns about.

## 2. The problem

**H2** — The course is recorded. It has been recorded for months.

- **Forty lessons is forty edits.** The first three are fine. Lesson eleven is where most
  courses quietly stop.
- **Lesson 30 does not match lesson 1.** Different day, different light, different energy —
  and a student notices the seam even when they cannot name it.
- **Production quality is what the refund email is about.** Nobody asks for their money
  back because the teaching was bad. They ask because it felt cheap.
- **Students stop watching in the middle.** Completion is the number that decides your
  reviews, your referrals, and whether the next launch works.

---

## 3. The solution

**H2** — Edited like a product, not like a post

**Paragraph 1** — A course is the one kind of video where the edit is not marketing. The
customer has already paid. What they receive is the video, and its quality is the quality
of the thing they bought.

**Paragraph 2** — Video Editor Agency edits the whole curriculum as one piece of work.
The same editor across every module, so lesson 40 matches lesson 1 — the same intro, the
same captions, the same treatment for every on-screen point. Revisions are genuinely
unlimited, which matters most on the module you re-record twice.

---

## 4. What we deliver

**H2** — What we edit for course creators

- **Full curriculum editing, module by module** — structured for completion, not just
  cut down.
- **A consistent look across every lesson** — the same intro, lower thirds and treatment
  from lesson one to the last.
- **Screen recordings and slides cut into the teaching** — readable at the size a student
  actually watches.
- **Motion graphics for the concepts that need showing** — the diagram that does the work
  a paragraph of narration cannot.
- **Launch and promo cuts** — the sales video and the trailer, from the course footage
  you already have.
- **Short-form clips from your teaching** — the free content that sells the paid content.
