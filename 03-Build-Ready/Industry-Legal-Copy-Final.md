## 2. The problem

**H2** — People choose a lawyer before they ever speak to one

- **The research happens without you.** By the time someone calls, they have watched three
  firms explain the same thing and decided which one sounded like they knew.
- **Your answer is forty minutes long.** It is accurate and complete and nobody watched
  past two.
- **Every firm's website video looks identical.** Same desk, same shelf of books, same
  music. Nothing in it says which firm this is.
- **Billable hours are the reason there is no content.** The video that would bring in
  three matters loses every week to the matters you already have.

---

## 3. The solution

**H2** — Authority is a thing you demonstrate, not a thing you assert

**Paragraph 1** — Legal marketing has one unusual property: the audience cannot evaluate
the expertise. Nobody hiring a lawyer can assess the law. What they can assess is whether
you explained something they were confused about — clearly, and without making them feel
stupid.

**Paragraph 2** — Video Editor Agency takes the recording and cuts it to the answer. The
long explanation becomes a watchable piece, the piece becomes a set of short-form answers,
and every one of them looks like it came from the same firm. Revisions are genuinely
unlimited, which matters when the wording of a point has to be exact.

---

## 4. What we deliver

**H2** — What we edit for legal practices

- **Educational explainers** — the common question answered once, properly, so it stops
  eating consultations.
- **Pieces to camera** — the recording tightened, restarts removed, the hesitations closed.
- **Short-form legal answers** — one question, one answer, cut vertical for Reels, TikTok
  and Shorts.
- **Firm and practitioner introductions** — who you are, for someone deciding whether to
  call.
- **On-screen text and motion graphics** — timelines, process steps, the sequence a client
  needs to see rather than hear.
- **Captions** — accurate, because a mis-transcribed legal term is worse than no caption.

## 7. ⚠️ One thing this page must never imply

**Nothing here says video wins cases, generates a specific number of enquiries, or
constitutes advice.** VEA edits video. Legal advertising rules vary by jurisdiction and
this page is read internationally — so the copy stays on what the edit does and never on
what the practice achieves.

*(Labelled: **INFERENCE**, T7, 2026-09-06. Advertising restrictions on lawyers are
jurisdictional and are not verified here. Not legal advice. The page as written makes no
