## 2. The problem

**H2**

> Four things that quietly cap a coaching business

- **The edit eats the week.** Hours that should go into coaching, curriculum, or sales calls go into cutting footage instead.
- **The course never launches.** The material is recorded. It sits on a drive because turning it into finished modules feels like starting a second business.
- **Posting is inconsistent.** Content goes out in bursts when there is time, and stops when there isn't — so the audience never builds momentum.
- **The production quality undercuts the price.** A programme sold at premium rates, presented in video that looks like it was assembled in a hurry, works against the rate you are asking for.
