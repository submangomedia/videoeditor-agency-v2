## 2. The problem

**H2** — Your expertise is the hardest thing to put on camera

- **The explanation takes twenty minutes and nobody watches twenty minutes.** The
  information is right. The format is losing.
- **Production quality reads as competence.** A patient deciding between two practices is
  not qualified to judge your medicine. They will judge what they can see.
- **The footage is recorded between clinics and it shows.** Interruptions, restarts, an
  answered phone — the good material is in there, buried in an hour of it.
- **The person who should be editing is you, and you have a list.** Which is why the
  channel has four videos and the last one is from March.

---

## 3. The solution

**H2** — The explanation you already give, made watchable

**Paragraph 1** — Patient education is the rare kind of video where the goal is
comprehension rather than reach. Someone came looking for an answer. Whether they get it —
and whether they trust the person giving it — is decided in the edit as much as in the
recording.

**Paragraph 2** — Video Editor Agency cuts the recording down to the answer, adds the
on-screen support the explanation needs, and holds the same treatment across every video
so the practice looks like one practice. Revisions are genuinely unlimited, which matters
when a clinical detail has to be worded exactly.

---

## 4. What we deliver

**H2** — What we edit for medical practices

- **Patient education videos** — the long explanation, cut to the length someone will
  actually watch.
- **Pieces to camera** — the recording tightened, the restarts removed, the pauses closed.
- **Motion graphics for the parts that need showing** — the diagram that does what
  narration cannot.
- **Clinic and practice content** — the introduction, the facility, the team.
- **Short-form answers** — the single common question, cut vertical for Reels, TikTok and
  Shorts.
- **Captions and on-screen terminology** — spelled correctly, and on screen long enough
  to read.
