## 2. The problem

**H2** — In aesthetics, the video is the consultation

- **The result is the whole pitch and the footage does not do it justice.** Different
  light, different angle, different day — and the change that is obvious in the room is
  invisible on camera.
- **Every clinic's feed looks the same.** The same treatment, the same stock line, the
  same music. A patient scrolling cannot tell any of you apart.
- **Ads get rejected and nobody knows which frame did it.** The creative was approved
  internally, and the platform disagreed.
- **The clinic is priced as premium and edited as budget.** Nothing undoes a treatment
  price faster than content that looks like it was made on a phone at the reception desk.

---

## 3. The solution

**H2** — Content that matches what the treatment costs

**Paragraph 1** — Aesthetics is a considered purchase. A patient researches for weeks,
compares clinics, and decides largely on what they can see. Your video is doing the work a
consultation would — before anyone has walked in.

**Paragraph 2** — Video Editor Agency edits the treatment content, the clinic film and the
short-form to one consistent standard, with the same editor across everything so the feed
reads as one clinic. Where results footage is involved, **we cut a compliant version
alongside the organic one**, so the campaign is not held up by a rejection. Revisions are
genuinely unlimited.

## 4. What we deliver

**H2** — What we edit for clinics

- **Treatment and procedure content** — the process explained, cut for a patient rather
  than a practitioner.
- **Results and transformation edits** — colour and exposure matched so the change reads
  as the change, not as the lighting.
- **Compliant ad variants** — a second cut built for platform review alongside the
  organic one. ⚠️ See §7.

- **Short-form for Reels, TikTok and Shorts** — cut vertical, captioned, one point each.

## 7. ⚠️ The compliance point, and why it is a selling point rather than a disclaimer

Before-and-after imagery is the default format in aesthetics, and **Meta's and Google's
advertising policies restrict it for health and beauty.** A clinic routinely produces a
results video that performs organically and is then rejected as an ad.

**Mango Media Digital already handles platform-compliance variants for clients** — this is
an operational fact about how the team works, not a theory. That is why "compliant ad
variants" is deliverable #3 rather than a footnote.

**The rule the page must hold:** *sell the edit, never the placement.* Producing a variant
built for review is something VEA controls. **Whether a platform approves it is not**, and
nothing on this page may imply a guarantee of approval.
