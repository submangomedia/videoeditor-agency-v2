## 2. The problem

**H2**

> Post-production is where agency video projects go to die

- **The edit is the bottleneck on every video you sell.** Strategy lands, the shoot lands, and then everything queues behind one person in a timeline.
- **Quality is uneven across your client roster.** Different editors on different accounts means different pacing and different standards — and your client sees your name on all of it.
- **An in-house edit team is expensive to build and impossible to shrink.** You staff for the peak and pay for it in the trough, or you staff for the trough and miss the peak.
- **Demand spikes faster than you can hire.** Campaign season arrives on a date you knew about, and the capacity still is not there.

## 7. ⚠️ The white-label problem

**This is the most important line on the page and its status is unknown.**

White-label appears twice in `videoeditor_agency_website_copy`, and **both times it is attached to a pricing tier that no longer exists**:

| Where | Exact wording |
|---|---|
| PAGE 8 body | *"White-label available on **Pro plan**."* |
| PAGE 8 deliverables | *"White-label delivery under your agency's brand name"* |
| Homepage §8, Pro tier | *"…Project manager \| **White-label available**"* |

**R04 ruled published per-unit rates.** Every retainer tier is dead — which takes the only stated condition on white-label with it. So there are two possibilities and the documents do not distinguish them:

1. **White-label is a real capability** that happened to be gated behind the top tier. Under per-unit rates it is simply available, and it belongs in the sub-headline and the deliverables.
2. **White-label was aspirational copy** written to make a $XXX tier look worth buying, and nobody has ever delivered under another agency's brand.

**These are not the same offer.** `website_copy` PAGE 3 lists eleven services and white-label is not among them — which leans toward (2), but leaning is not knowing.

**T7 will not guess.** An agency that signs on the strength of white-label and then finds it is not real is a lost client and a reputational problem in a small market. `instructions.md` §5 rule 1: no placeholder a client could quote back at you.

**What Masud must answer:** *Has Video Editor Agency ever delivered work under a client agency's brand, with no VEA marking on the file or the delivery? Would you commit to it on the site?*
